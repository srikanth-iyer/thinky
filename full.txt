thinky is a small, fast outliner for your notes. Write in bullets, link pages together and keep a daily journal, all in plain Markdown files in a folder you choose.

Write and link
• A journal page for every day, ready when you open the app
• Pages, [[links]], #tags and properties
• Linked references: every note that mentions a page, shown under it
• To-dos with scheduled and deadline dates
• Quick search that forgives typos in page names

Your notes stay yours
• Plain Markdown files on your own device. No account, no servers, no ads, no tracking.
• To use the same notes on your phone and computer, keep the folder in a sync app you already use, such as Google Drive or Syncthing.
• Safe with sync: if a page changed on another device while you were writing, thinky merges the two, or shows both versions side by side. Nothing is silently overwritten.
• Safety copies and each page's history let you go back to an earlier version.

Works with Logseq folders
thinky opens an existing Logseq Markdown folder as it is, with its settings, and writes back the lines you didn't touch exactly as they were. You can keep using both.

Quick capture on Android
• Home-screen widgets for today's journal and a one-tap "+"
• A Quick Settings tile and "Add to journal" in the share sheet
• "Add to thinky" in the text selection menu

Many looks, light and dark
Pick a day look and a night look, or follow the system.

Why it needs "All files access"
thinky reads and writes your notes folder where your sync app keeps it, so the same plain files work on every device. It only opens the folder you choose.

thinky is open source (AGPL). Feedback and ideas are welcome on GitHub.
